RBI/NPCI GUIDELINES: IMMEDIATE PAYMENT SERVICE (IMPS)

1. AVAILABILITY
IMPS, operated by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), is
available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year — including bank
holidays, Sundays, and RTGS/NEFT non-processing windows. IMPS was one of the
first real-time, always-on retail payment rails in India, launched in 2010,
well before NEFT and RTGS moved to 24x7 operation.

2. TRANSACTION LIMITS
- Minimum amount: Re 1.
- Maximum amount: Rs 5,00,000 (Rs 5 lakh) per transaction, as capped by
  NPCI; individual banks may set a lower per-transaction or per-day limit
  based on the customer's risk profile and channel (mobile banking, internet
  banking, ATM, SMS, USSD).

3. MMID (MOBILE MONEY IDENTIFIER)
MMID is a 7-digit number issued by a bank to a customer for use with mobile
banking, uniquely identifying the bank and account combination when paired
with a registered mobile number. It allows a sender to transfer funds using
only the beneficiary's mobile number + MMID, without needing to know the
full account number or IFSC.

4. TRANSFER MODES
IMPS supports three primary beneficiary-addressing modes:
  - P2A (Person-to-Account): Account number + IFSC — no MMID required.
  - P2P (Person-to-Person / Mobile+MMID): Registered mobile number + MMID.
  - P2P (Aadhaar-based): Aadhaar number linked to a bank account (less
    commonly used since UPI's growth).
This platform implements Account+IFSC and Mobile+MMID transfer modes.

5. RRN (RETRIEVAL REFERENCE NUMBER) FORMAT
Every IMPS transaction is assigned a 12-digit numeric Retrieval Reference
Number (RRN) generated by NPCI, used to trace and reconcile the transaction
across the remitting bank, NPCI's IMPS switch, and the beneficiary bank.
Format: 12 digits, e.g., 302501234567. The RRN must be quoted in all dispute
and reconciliation requests raised with NPCI.

6. IMPS CHARGES STRUCTURE
NPCI prescribes an interchange fee structure between banks based on
transaction value slabs (e.g., different interchange for <Rs 1,000,
Rs 1,000-Rs 25,000, Rs 25,000-Rs 1,00,000, Rs 1,00,000-Rs 2,00,000, and
Rs 2,00,000-Rs 5,00,000 bands). Customer-facing charges are set by the
remitting bank and vary; many banks now offer free IMPS on digital channels
similar to NEFT, though this is not an RBI mandate the way NEFT online
charges are.

7. RESPONSE TIME
IMPS is designed for near-instant settlement — funds are expected to reflect
in the beneficiary account within seconds of the remitter confirming the
transaction, since it uses real-time, transaction-by-transaction processing
through the NPCI switch rather than batch settlement.
